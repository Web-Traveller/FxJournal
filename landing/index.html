Wall Street P&L Calendar

An 8-column layout with weekly ribbon KPI subtotals, win-rate metrics, daily net P&L heatmaps, and expandable day execution logs.

Prop Firm Risk Guard

Real-time daily loss tracking and maximum trailing drawdown alerts configured to FTMO, The Funded Trader, and custom prop firm evaluation rules.

100% Local-First Privacy

Zero cloud databases, zero telemetry, and no broker credentials needed. Your trade notes, account balances, and screenshots live strictly on your NVMe/SSD.

Detailed Institutional Logging

Capture entry/exit prices, fees, slippage, trade session tags (London/NY), setups, and multi-image screenshot attachments with full-screen lightbox inspection.

Cryptographic Auto-Updater

Powered by Tauri v2 and Minisign Ed25519 signatures. FxJournal quietly verifies releases in the background, keeping your journal up to date seamlessly.

One-Click Portable Backups

Export and restore complete databases anytime with encrypted .fxbackup JSON archives. Effortlessly transfer your data between trading rigs.
